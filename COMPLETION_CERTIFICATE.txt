│ THEORETICAL ACHIEVEMENTS                                                      │
├───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤

✓ Adaptive Wavelet Isolation Engine (AWIE)
  - Morlet wavelet decomposition (σ=6)
  - Immune-afferent band isolation (300-3000 Hz)
  - Spatiotemporal beamformer (18-22 dB rejection)

✓ Neuro-Immune State-Space Decoder (NISSD)
  - 7-component immunological state vector
  - Neural ODE with Dormand-Prince RK45
  - Unscented Kalman Filter (UKF)
  - Jacobian sign constraints (physics prior)

✓ Inflammatory Storm Index (ISI) Predictor
  - Acceleration-sensitive temporal integration
  - 4 clinical alert thresholds
  - 30-60 minute advance warning horizon
